# ArmoryWiki

An encyclopedia of firearms: their designs, the people and organizations behind them, the cartridges they fire and how they work.

## Firearms and their versions

**Firearm**:
A distinct design, known under one primary name. A rival design for the same requirement is a separate firearm (MKb 42(W) vs the StG 44), and so is a design made in another country, including a licensed copy. The design decides, not the name.
_Avoid_: weapon, gun, model

**Variant**:
A changed version of a firearm's design from the same origin, such as a prototype, a pre-series run (MKb 42(H) for the StG 44) or a suffixed revision (M4A1, M16A2).
_Avoid_: version, sub-model, model

**Base variant**:
The variant that the firearm's unsuffixed name refers to, normally the first adopted or production version.

**Alternative name**:
Another name for the same, unchanged design, such as a later redesignation (MP 43 and MP 44 for the StG 44) or a common nickname.
_Avoid_: alias, AKA

## Dates and places

**Design date**:
When a design reached the form the firearm is known by: a finished prototype or a frozen design. Distinct from adoption and from the start of production.
_Avoid_: inception, introduction date

**Country of origin**:
The state that existed where and when a firearm or cartridge was designed, never a modern successor (the StG 44 comes from Nazi Germany, not Germany).
_Avoid_: origin country, home country

**Nationality**:
The state a person was a citizen of during their principal design work.
_Avoid_: citizenship

## People and organizations

**Designer**:
A person or organization credited with a firearm's design, in one of three roles: **lead designer** (originated the design), **co-designer** (worked with the lead from the start) or **developer** (took over or substantially reworked an existing design).
_Avoid_: inventor, creator, refiner

**Organization**:
A company, design bureau or state arsenal. Its country is the state it is headquartered in today, or was when it ceased to exist.
_Avoid_: company (when a bureau or arsenal is meant), firm

**Manufacturer**:
An organization that produced a firearm, under its own design or under license.
_Avoid_: maker, producer

**Adoption**:
A formal decision by a state's armed forces or a government service, police included, to take a firearm into service. Use by non-state groups is not an adoption.
_Avoid_: user, service use

## How firearms relate

**Derived from**:
The firearm was developed directly from the other firearm's design. Derivation never loops: a firearm cannot be derived, through any chain, from itself. The same holds for *copy of*.
_Avoid_: based on, descendant

**Copy of**:
The firearm reproduces the other's design with at most minor changes, under license or not (the Type 56 is a copy of the AK-47). Real design changes make it *derived from* instead.
_Avoid_: clone, knock-off

**Influenced by**:
The firearm's design shows a documented influence from the other, without direct derivation.
_Avoid_: inspired by

**Competed against**:
Both firearms were entered in the same official trial or competition.

**Replaced**:
The firearm succeeded the other in service of its country of origin. Replacements elsewhere belong to that country's adoption.
_Avoid_: superseded, successor

## Classification

**Operating mechanism**:
How a firearm uses energy, from the shooter or the cartridge, to cycle its action. Mechanisms form a tree, and a firearm belongs to every mechanism above its own. How the breech locks (rotating bolt, toggle lock) is a different property and not part of the operating mechanism.
_Avoid_: action type, operating system, locking mechanism

**Tag**:
A label from a fixed vocabulary that classifies a firearm by type (assault rifle, carbine), role (sniper) or era. A firearm can carry several.
_Avoid_: category

**Era tag**:
A tag for a period in which the firearm saw significant service, regardless of when it was designed.

**Firing mode**:
What one trigger pull does on a self-loading firearm: **semi-automatic** (one shot), **burst** (a fixed number of shots) or **automatic** (continuous fire). Manually operated firearms have no firing modes.
_Avoid_: fire selector setting, "single"

## Specifications

**Empty weight**:
Weight without magazine and ammunition. A weight whose source does not say what it includes is not an empty weight; it is kept as an unspecified weight.
_Avoid_: unloaded weight, weight

**Overall length**:
Length with any folding or telescoping stock extended. The folded length is recorded separately.

**Rate of fire**:
The cyclic rate: how fast the mechanism fires in continuous fire, regardless of reloading.
_Avoid_: practical rate of fire

**Effective range**:
The point-target range stated by the adopting military.

**Standard magazine capacity**:
The number of rounds in the standard-issue magazine.

## Ammunition

**Cartridge**:
A specific ammunition type, such as 7.62×39mm, defined by its case and bullet. Firearms are chambered for cartridges.
_Avoid_: caliber, round, ammo

**Parent cartridge**:
The cartridge whose case another cartridge was developed from. Parent links never loop.

**Primary cartridge**:
The cartridge a variant is chiefly chambered for, when it accepts more than one.

## Sources and evidence

**Source**:
A published work facts are taken from: a book, article, website, patent, manual, video or dataset (such as Wikidata).
_Avoid_: reference, bibliography entry

**Citation**:
A link from one firearm, variant, person, organization or cartridge, optionally one of its fields, to a source, with a locator such as a page, claim number, timestamp or deep link.
_Avoid_: footnote (that is how a citation is displayed), reference

**Image**:
A picture of a firearm, person, organization or cartridge, always with its author, license and where it came from. The first image of a subject is its thumbnail.
_Avoid_: media, photo, picture

**License**:
The terms an image may be used under. Only public domain, CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA are accepted.
_Avoid_: copyright, rights

**Video**:
An embedded YouTube video about a firearm. Videos found automatically start as **suggested** and become **approved** or **rejected** by an admin; only approved videos are shown. A rejected suggestion is never suggested again; a deleted one may be.
_Avoid_: video reference, clip

## Editing and publishing

**Admin**:
A person allowed to create, edit, publish and delete content. There is one role; public visitors cannot edit.
_Avoid_: editor, moderator, user

**Draft**:
Content that exists but is not yet shown publicly, such as freshly imported firearms awaiting review.

**Published**:
Content visible on the public site.

**Publish requirements**:
What must be filled in before content can be published. A firearm needs a country of origin, design date, operating mechanism and a base variant whose primary cartridge is published; a cartridge needs its type and country of origin; people and organizations only need a name.
_Avoid_: publish gate, validation

**Unpublish**:
Returning published content to draft, which removes it from the public site.

**Locked field**:
A field an admin has changed by hand. Imports never overwrite it until an admin unlocks it.
_Avoid_: protected field, override

**Import**:
Bringing facts from an outside source (Wikidata, Wikimedia Commons, Wikipedia, YouTube) into the encyclopedia, matched by the source's own identifier. An import creates drafts, refreshes unlocked fields of drafts and only fills empty fields of published content. It never publishes, unpublishes or deletes anything; what disappeared from the source is reported for an admin instead.
_Avoid_: sync, scrape
